# MediNova Health Companion

Build a complete, modern, premium full-stack web application called “MediNova AI – AI-Powered Healthcare Diagnosis Assistance”.

The website should look like a real-world healthcare startup/product, NOT like a basic college project. It should be visually impressive, highly responsive, interactive, accessible, and designed around AI-powered healthcare assistance.

CORE CONCEPT

MediNova AI is an AI-powered healthcare assistance platform that helps users:

- Understand their symptoms

- Identify possible health concerns

- Ask intelligent follow-up questions

- Understand what they should do next

- Detect potentially urgent warning signs

- Explain medical reports in simple language

- Compare previous and current reports

- Understand medicines and possible interactions

- Track symptoms and health trends

- Maintain a personal health timeline

- Prepare questions and summaries for doctor visits

- Track general wellness

- Receive preventive health reminders

IMPORTANT: The platform is a healthcare decision-support and education system. It must NEVER claim to provide a confirmed medical diagnosis or replace a qualified healthcare professional.

5. AI SYMPTOM CHECKER

This must be one of the most important features.

Create a beautiful step-by-step symptom assessment interface.

Allow users to enter symptoms naturally.

Example:

“I have fever, headache and body pain since yesterday.”

Support:

- Text input

- Voice input

- English

- Hindi

- Hinglish

AI should identify:

- Symptoms

- Duration

- Severity

- Location

- Frequency

- Associated symptoms

Then ask intelligent follow-up questions.

Example:

“Where exactly is the pain?”

“When did it start?”

“Rate your pain from 1–10.”

“Do you have vomiting?”

“Are you experiencing breathing difficulty?”

Do not immediately provide a diagnosis.

After gathering enough information show:

POSSIBLE HEALTH CONCERNS

Display multiple possible causes.

For each:

- Why it may be relevant

- Matching symptoms

- Missing information

- Important warning signs

Use wording such as:

“Your symptoms may be associated with...”

Never say:

“You definitely have...”
